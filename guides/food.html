Eating Well on a Student Budget

Your first year is busy; new classes , new friends, and long days on campus . It is easy to skip or spend ypur whole allowance on milk tea. Eating regular meals helps you focus on class, and you do not need a big buget to do it.

Where to Eat Near Campus

you have more options than you think. Have the usual go-to spots:

  • The school canteen for quick rice meals between classes
  • Carinderias outside the gate for cheap, filling ulam and rice.
  • Your own baon from home, Which is the cheapest choice of all
  • Convenience stores for emergency snacks, not for every meal.

How to Plan Your Weekly Food Budget.

A little planning on Sundays saves you from being broke by Thursday.

  1. Check how much allowanve you have for the whole week.
  2. Set aside money for fare and school needs first.
  3. Divide what what is left by the number of school days.
  4. Pack baon on the last two days to stretch your budget.
  5. Keep a small amount for emergencies, like a suprice project.
